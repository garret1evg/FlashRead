Effective date: 31 August 2026
Last updated: 6 September 2026

No accounts

FlashReader does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or password to use the app. We do not operate a user registration system for FlashReader.

If you contact us by email, we will receive the address and message you send so we can reply.

Local data

FlashReader stores the following information in the app’s storage on your device so the library, reader, and settings can work:

  • Books and files you import (for example EPUB, FB2, FB2.ZIP, TXT, Markdown, and HTML)
  • Texts you write or paste in the app
  • Extracted text used for reading and speed reading
  • Titles and optional cover images
  • Bookmarks, reading position, and reading history
  • Reader and speed-reading settings (for example theme, font size, words per minute)

This library content stays on your device unless you export it or include it in a device backup.

Content and technical data

FlashReader treats your reading materials and the technical data used to operate analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and billing as separate categories.

How FlashReader treats content versus technical data
Category Examples Where it goes
Library and reading content Book files, written or pasted text, covers, bookmarks, reading history Stored on your device. Not sent to Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob.
Technical and advertising data Usage events, crash reports, device information, identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, purchase tokens Processed by Google services such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, UMP, and Google Play Billing, as described in this policy.

FlashReader does not upload your imported books, texts you write, cover images, bookmarks, or reading history to Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob. Analytics events and crash reports are designed so they do not include book titles or the text of your materials.

Firebase Analytics

FlashReader uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Firebase is provided by Google. Collection is subject to your consent choices where Google’s consent tools apply.

Firebase Analytics may process:

  • Events — for example importing a file (format and result, not the file itself), opening the reader, starting or finishing a speed-reading session, and changing settings
  • Device information — such as device model, operating system, language, and app version
  • Identifiers — such as an app instance identifier and, where permitted, the device advertising ID
  • Approximate geolocation — typically inferred from IP address, not from device GPS. FlashReader does not request location permission for this purpose
  • Diagnostics — technical information about the app session that helps Google operate Analytics

These events are not intended to include the titles or full text of your books. Google may use this technical data according to Google’s own policies.

Firebase Crashlytics

FlashReader uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and error reports so we can find and fix stability problems. Crashlytics is provided by Google. Collection is subject to your consent choices where Google’s consent tools apply.

Firebase Crashlytics may process:

  • Crash and error reports — stack traces, the type of failure (crash, non-fatal error, or ANR), and related diagnostic logs
  • Device and app information — such as device model, operating system, language, and app version
  • Identifiers — such as an installation identifier used to group reports from the same app install

These reports are not intended to include the titles or full text of your books. Google may use this technical data according to Google’s own policies.

Google AdMob

FlashReader may show advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob is provided by Google. Ads are requested only when applicable consent requirements allow it.

Google AdMob may process:

  • IP address
  • Approximate location (often derived from IP address)
  • Advertising ID and other advertising identifiers
  • Ad interactions (for example impressions and clicks)
  • Device and app information used to serve and measure ads

AdMob does not receive your imported books, written texts, covers, bookmarks, or reading history from FlashReader.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

In regions where Google requires a consent message (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), FlashReader uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your choices about analytics, crash reporting, and advertising cookies or similar technologies.

  • Your consent or refusal is stored and used to decide whether Analytics, Crashlytics, and ads may run.
  • If Google requires a privacy options entry point, you can change your choices later from Manage Privacy in FlashReader settings.
  • UMP is a Google service. Google may process the consent interaction as needed to show the form and honor the result.

Google Play Billing

Optional purchases, such as removing ads, are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your full payment card details. Google handles payment information under Google Play’s terms and privacy policy.

To recognize a purchase on your device, FlashReader may receive and store a purchase token and related product status from Google Play. That information is used to unlock the feature you bought and to restore a purchase on the same Google account when you ask the app to restore it.

Legal bases (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process personal data only where a legal basis applies. Depending on the activity, that basis may be:

Legal bases we may rely on
Purpose Legal basis
Provide the app on your device, store your library locally, and honor a purchase through Google Play Performance of a contract (providing the app you chose to install and any in-app purchase you make)
Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and advertising technologies that require consent Consent, collected through Google UMP where required. You may withdraw consent in Settings → Manage Privacy
Respond to support or privacy requests you send us Legitimate interests in answering you, or steps at your request before or after a contract
Comply with law, Play policies, or a binding request Legal obligation, where one applies

Google acts as an independent controller or as a processor for some of the services above. Google’s legal bases are described in Google’s own documentation.

How long data is kept

  • Local library and settings remain on your device until you delete a specific item, clear app storage, or uninstall FlashReader (subject to any copies in a device backup you control).
  • Purchase status remains available through Google Play for as long as Google associates the purchase with your Google account, and locally while the app can read that status.
  • Firebase Analytics data is retained by Google for 14 months.
  • Firebase Crashlytics crash data is retained by Google for 90 days.
  • AdMob and Google Play retain data according to Google’s policies, which we do not control.
  • Email you send us is kept as long as needed to handle your request and any related legal or accounting needs.

How to delete your data

You can remove local FlashReader data by deleting items from your library or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling FlashReader deletes the app’s local storage on that device, except copies that may remain in an operating-system or cloud backup you created.

Uninstalling the app or deleting local files does not guarantee that Google will delete information already processed by Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, UMP, or Google Play Billing. Those services are operated by Google. You may also use Google account advertising and privacy controls, and you may contact us at yevhen.chmutov.support@gmail.com if you want help identifying what we can request or delete.

Security

Local library data is stored in the app’s sandbox on your device. Network communication used by FlashReader and by Google’s SDKs is sent over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International processing

Google may process technical, crash, advertising, consent, and billing data on servers in the United States and other countries. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from the laws in your country. Where required, Google states that it uses approved transfer mechanisms, such as Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google’s privacy documentation for details.

If you contact us by email, your message may be stored or read in Poland.

Children

FlashReader is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal data, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (for example a data protection authority in the EEA, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, or the competent authority in Switzerland).

Because FlashReader does not create accounts, most library data exists only on your device and you can delete it there. For data processed by Google, you may need to use Google’s tools or ask us to help where we can. Contact yevhen.chmutov.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. The updated policy applies when it is posted. If a change is material, we will take additional steps required by law, such as providing a notice in the app or on this site.
