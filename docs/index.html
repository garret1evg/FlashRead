FlashReader

FlashReader is an Android app for reading your own books and texts at your own pace — including speed reading with adjustable words per minute.

Import files into a local library, continue where you left off, or paste text for a quick session. Books and documents stay on your device; you do not create an account to use the app.

FlashReader will be available on Google Play.

Supported formats

  • EPUB

    Import EPUB books into your library and read them in the app.

  • FB2 and FB2.ZIP

    Open FictionBook files, including compressed FB2.ZIP archives.

  • TXT

    Import plain-text files or write your own material in the editor.

  • Markdown

    Read Markdown documents alongside your other imported texts.

  • HTML

    Import HTML files when you want to keep a local copy of a page or article.

  • YouTube transcripts

    Load a transcript from a YouTube video URL and speed-read it in FlashReader.
